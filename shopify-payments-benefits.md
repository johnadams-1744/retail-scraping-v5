# Shopify Payments — Key Benefits for Merchant Outreach

Compiled for cross-sell reps positioning Shopify Payments to existing Shopify merchants. All stats sourced from Shopify first-party data and industry reporting (2025-2026).

---

## Top Talking Points

1. **Eliminate the third-party transaction fee surcharge.** Shopify charges an extra 0.5%-2.0% on every order processed through an external gateway. Switching to Shopify Payments removes that fee entirely — on a store doing $500K/yr, that's $2,500-$10,000 back in the merchant's pocket annually.

2. **Checkout conversion jumps up to 36%.** Shop Pay (included with Shopify Payments) delivers a 36% average conversion uplift vs. standard checkout flows. It's 4x faster than a regular checkout and converts at 1.72x the rate of non-Shop Pay checkout — even higher on mobile.

3. **Free fraud & chargeback protection with Shopify Protect.** Eligible Shop Pay orders are automatically covered by Shopify Protect at no extra cost. Shopify reimburses the full order cost *and* the chargeback fee on fraudulent disputes, and handles the dispute process for the merchant. Shopify Payments merchants already win 45% more chargeback disputes than those on third-party providers.

4. **Average order value increases up to 50% with Shop Pay Installments.** Offering buy-now-pay-later (powered by Affirm) through Shop Pay Installments has been shown to lift AOV by up to 50% and cut cart abandonment by up to 28%. Merchants get paid in full upfront — zero risk.

5. **100M+ Shop Pay buyers ready to convert.** Shop Pay has over 100 million registered buyers. When a shopper already has Shop Pay saved, they get a one-click checkout on the merchant's store — removing friction that kills conversion everywhere else.

6. **Unified commerce: online + in-person in one place.** Shopify Payments is the only platform offering fully integrated online and POS payments. Inventory, orders, customer data, and financial reporting all live in a single dashboard — no reconciliation headaches across multiple providers.

7. **Competitive processing rates with no hidden costs.** Rates start at 2.9% + $0.30 (Basic) and drop to 2.5% + $0.30 (Advanced). These are all-in — no gateway fees, no monthly payment-provider charges, no surprise line items. Chargeback fees are $15 and refunded if the merchant wins.

8. **Accept every way customers want to pay.** Shopify Payments supports all major credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, Shop Pay Installments, and local currencies — all enabled from a single setup. No juggling multiple provider integrations.

9. **Enterprise-grade security out of the box.** Full PCI compliance, 3D Secure authentication, payment data encryption, and ML-powered fraud detection are built in. Merchants don't need to manage security certifications or bolt on third-party fraud tools.

10. **One-click setup, immediate activation.** No lengthy underwriting or third-party onboarding. Merchants can activate Shopify Payments directly in their admin and start processing the same day.
